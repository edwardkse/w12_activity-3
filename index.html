Mission and Vision: Words into Strategy

Note: This is a group activity. Groups must first align internally.

1 Choose a Brand

  • Select one brand you admire or frequently use.
  • Note down why you admire this brand.

2 The Brand's Mission & Vision

  • Provide the official Mission Statement and Vision Statement of your chosen brand.

3 Analyse & Critique

Mission Statement

  • What does the brand claim it does today?
  • Is it clear, specific, and realistic? Or vague and generic? Justify.
  • Does it match what you, as a customer, experience? Give an example.

Vision Statement

  • What future does the brand aspire to?
  • Is it ambitious and inspiring, or generic and unrealistic? Justify.
  • Do you believe in it? Why or why not?

4 Strategic Interpretation

Strategic Element Group Interpretation
Core purpose (Mission) What must never change?
Long-term ambition (Vision) What must eventually be true?
Key values What behaviours must advertising reflect?
Strategic boundaries What would be "off-brand"?

5 Strategic Alignment

Strategic Focus

  • Identify ONE primary strategic focus for the advertising effort.
  • Clearly state whether this focus is driven mainly by the mission or the vision.

Key Strategic Insight

  • What belief must the audience adopt?
  • What tension exists between where the brand is now and where it wants to be?

Note: This insight must logically connect to the mission or vision—not assumptions or trends.
